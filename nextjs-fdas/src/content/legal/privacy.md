# Privacy

**Effective date:** May 9, 2026  
**Last updated:** May 9, 2026

## What this site is

AceAnalytics.dev is a personal portfolio and demonstration site operated by an individual based in Birmingham, Alabama. It exists primarily as a hobby project to showcase financial-analysis tooling to industry peers, prospective collaborators, and recruiters. It is not a commercial software-as-a-service offering, and the demonstrations on this site are not intended for use with live production data.

This Privacy notice explains what limited information is collected when you visit the site or interact with its demos, and what is done with that information.

## Information collected

**Information you provide.** If you book a demo through cal.aceanalytics.dev, send an email to a hello@, press@, or similar address, or fill in any contact form, you provide your name, email address, employer (if disclosed), and any message content. If you upload sample documents to one of the demonstration tools (Aperture, Parallax, Lattice, or Dialect), the contents of those uploads are processed transiently to generate the demo output.

**Information collected automatically.** Standard server logs and privacy-respecting analytics record IP address (truncated where feasible), user agent, referring page, and pages visited. These logs are used to monitor uptime, debug issues, and understand aggregate traffic patterns.

**Cookies.** The site uses essential cookies required for navigation and session state, and may set a single first-party analytics cookie. No third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

## How information is used

Information is used only to: respond to inquiries you initiate; schedule and run demonstrations; operate, secure, and improve the site; and comply with legal obligations.

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. Information is not used to train third-party machine-learning models.

## Demo uploads

Files you upload to the demonstration tools are processed only to produce the requested output. By default, uploads are deleted within 24 hours unless you explicitly request otherwise. Do not upload material that contains personally identifiable information of third parties, customer financial records, or any non-public information you are not authorized to share. The site is a showcase environment, not a controlled production system.

## Third-party services

The site relies on a small number of third parties to function: a hosting provider, an email provider, and a calendar/booking provider (Google Calendar). Each receives only the information necessary to deliver its service. Their own privacy practices govern the data they handle.

## Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have provided by emailing hello@aceanalytics.dev. Reasonable requests will be honored within thirty (30) days. Residents of jurisdictions with applicable privacy laws (including California under the CCPA/CPRA and the EU/UK under GDPR) have additional rights, which will be honored where they apply.

You may opt out of analytics by enabling Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser; the site honors both signals.

## Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable threshold), and information is not knowingly collected from them.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. No internet-facing system is perfectly secure; you are encouraged to keep what you share to what is necessary for the conversation.

## Changes

This notice may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

Questions about this notice should be directed to **hello@aceanalytics.dev**.
