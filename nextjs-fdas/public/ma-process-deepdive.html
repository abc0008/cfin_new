OP_M2 · THE GATE

What the score triggersgate · continue / pause / narrow

The weighted score maps to one of four diligence decisions — the Phase 1 gate.

score → decision
4.0-5.0
Continue — prioritizeStrong fit; advance to deep diligence with priority.
3.0-3.9
Continue with conditionsAdvance, but narrow to the value-driving questions.
2.0-2.9
Narrow or pauseMaterial concerns; reduce scope or pause for evidence.
<2.0
No-bid / deferFit or risk fails the thesis; defer or decline.

The score doesn’t decide the deal — it decides how much diligence, where. Green prioritizes; red defers before the team sinks cost into a poor-fit target.
